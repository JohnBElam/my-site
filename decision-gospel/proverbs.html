Decision Gospel / The Proverbs The Book of Wisdom

The Proverbs

One line. One truth. No deck required.

I

The decision not named is the decision not made. The decision not made is the decision made by default.

II

Data without a question is just storage with ambition.

III

The skip is fastest at the start and most expensive at the end.

IV

The dashboard that is always green is lying.

V

The question floats up and away if no one catches it. Catch it before the laptop opens.

VI

Speed without a frame is just arriving at the wrong place faster.

VII

Motion is not progress. The sprint proves nothing about the destination.

VIII

You can iterate to a better solution. You cannot iterate to the right problem.

IX

The analyst who starts at Step Four will end at Step Four. The problem stays at Step One.

X

The frame you borrow answers the problem you did not have.

XI

The stakeholder ignored at the start will be unavoidable at the end.

XII

Build for the decision maker you have, not the one you wish you had.

XIII

The meeting that ends without a decision was not a meeting. It was a rehearsal.

XIV

The person who says wait before the data loads is not slowing the work. They are finding it.

XV

Karaoke knowledge sounds like expertise until the music stops.

XVI

Every orphaned system was once someone's priority.

XVII

A decision system without a steward is a clock nobody checks.

XVIII

The model that is right but unused is indistinguishable from the model that was never built.

XIX

A pilot that never scales is a lesson, if you learned it. A failure, if you didn't.

XX

The skip is how you build the bridge to nowhere.

XXI

Uncertainty named is a discipline. Uncertainty hidden is a liability.

XXII

False precision is dishonesty with better formatting.

XXIII

Adoptable beats optimal. It has always beaten optimal.

XXIV

The sunk cost is not a reason to continue. It is a reason the room will not want to stop.

XXV

A model with no owner is a decision with no future.

Each one a stone. You only need one.
